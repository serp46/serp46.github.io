Brain APP privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Brain APP is an internal tool used by the marketing team to manage the Facebook and Instagram pages and ad accounts of the businesses it works for: publishing posts, replying to and moderating comments, and reading page and ad statistics.

  • Data accessed: page content, public comments on those pages, page and ad statistics, through the Meta Graph API with the page owners' authorisation.
  • Use: page management and internal reporting only.
  • Sharing: no data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Storage: access tokens are kept in the operator's system keychain; reports are stored locally. Comment author names are not stored.
  • Data deletion: to request deletion of any data related to you, see data deletion instructions.
