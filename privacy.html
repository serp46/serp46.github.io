ads-ai privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

ads-ai is an internal tool. It is used only by its owner, Paul Sergentu, to access Google Ads accounts he is authorised to manage.

  • Data accessed: Google Ads account and campaign reporting data, through the Google Ads API with the owner's consent.
  • Use: internal analysis and reporting for the owner only.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • Storage: credentials are kept in the owner's local operating system keychain; reports are stored locally.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: through the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
